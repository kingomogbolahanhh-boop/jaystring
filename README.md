# JAYSTRINGS Studio

Build a complete premium editorial production website for JAYSTRINGS® — a sonic architecture studio and creative house with the tagline "Sound Without Limits." and core statement "We build records. We don't sell beats." 

Aesthetic & Tone:
- High-end agency design combining luxury editorial typography, cinema, architectural grid precision, and modern African creative heritage.
- Sophisticated dark/refined palette with editorial serif & grotesque typography contrasts, asymmetric brutalist/editorial grids, stark negative space, and tactile subtle motion.
- Absolutely no generic music clip art, cheesy waveforms, or stock gradient fluff.

Key Sections & Features:
1. Editorial Hero: Bold typographic treatment for JAYSTRINGS®, tagline "Sound Without Limits.", manifesto teaser, and cinema-grade layout.
2. The Manifesto / Philosophy ("Sonic Architecture"): Explaining "We build records. We don't sell beats" — artist development, sound shaping, and emotional intelligence in composition.
3. Selected Discography & Sound Library: Minimalist bespoke audio player with custom tracks, credits (Artist, Role: Production / Engineering / Arranging), metadata, and seamless play/pause controls.
4. Services & Disciplines: Sonic Architecture, Record Production, Film & Cinematic Scoring, Artist Development, Mixing & Spatial Audio Engineering.
5. Studio & Process / The Laboratory: A look inside the creative philosophy, equipment ethos, and global African sonic narrative.
6. Press, Accolades & Quotes: High-fashion editorial quotes and industry recognition.
7. Private Inquiries & Production Bookings: An elevated, selective inquiry form for labels, artists, and film directors.
8. Interactive details: Smooth audio previewing, refined hover states, elegant cursor/motion interactions, and full responsive design.

# JAYSTRINGS® Editorial Production Website

## Vision
Create a complete, premium single-page studio experience that feels like an independent culture magazine crossed with a film title sequence: dark, exacting, tactile, and distinctly contemporary African without leaning on clichés.

## Experience
- Open with an oversized JAYSTRINGS® wordmark, “Sound Without Limits.”, a restrained manifesto teaser, and a cinematic original studio image.
- Build a precise editorial rhythm using asymmetric grids, ruled lines, issue-style numbering, generous negative space, and serif/grotesque contrast.
- Keep navigation compact and anchored to the major sections, with a focused mobile menu.
- Use subtle entrance, image-reveal, cursor, hover, and marquee motion while respecting reduced-motion settings.

## Sections
1. **Editorial Hero** — immediate brand statement, manifesto language, and direct links to selected work and private inquiries.
2. **Sonic Architecture** — the “We build records. We don’t sell beats.” philosophy, with artist development, sound shaping, and emotional composition principles.
3. **Selected Works** — a bespoke audio desk with a single-active-track player, play/pause, progress, duration, track metadata, and production credits.
4. **Disciplines** — Sonic Architecture, Record Production, Film & Cinematic Scoring, Artist Development, and Mixing & Spatial Audio Engineering.
5. **The Laboratory** — original studio imagery, process stages, equipment ethos, and the global African sonic narrative.
6. **Press & Recognition** — editorial pull quotes and a restrained accolade index.
7. **Private Inquiries** — a polished form for artists, labels, and film directors with project type, timeline, budget range, and creative brief fields.
8. **Footer** — brand statement, location/availability language, social links, and compact navigation.

## Content and Assets
- Generate a cohesive set of cinematic studio photographs rather than using stock imagery or music clip art.
- Use tasteful sample project names, credits, quotes, and recognition entries where verified JAYSTRINGS® details were not supplied; make them easy to replace.
- Use locally generated browser audio textures for functional previews, avoiding unlicensed external tracks.
- Do not add persistence or email delivery; inquiry submission will validate and show a refined confirmation state in the page.

## Technical Details
- Implement the complete experience at `/` with semantic sections and one H1.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph text, and Twitter metadata.
- Define the full semantic palette, typography, spacing, and motion language in the design system; load editorial fonts through document head links.
- Build focused React components for navigation, audio controls, content sections, and inquiry form.
- Ensure keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive image text, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from mobile through wide desktop.
- Verify compilation, runtime behavior, audio interaction, form states, and key desktop/mobile viewport screenshots.
